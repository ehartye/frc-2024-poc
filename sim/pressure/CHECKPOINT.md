# Pressure-only checkpoint

`checkpoint.export(plant)` and `checkpoint.restore(record)` preserve chassis, NOTE, rotor states, elapsed time, cumulative energy ledger and initial energy without resetting contact energy. Exact source/configuration/grid/material-phase identity is required. Reconstructed potential, kinetic energy and residual must match. The digest detects corruption, not maliciously forged provenance; this is a local numerical checkpoint, not an attestation of physical validity.

Four tests cover fresh-state short split versus uninterrupted byte parity, purity/corruption and malformed/legacy/changed-law rejection. Contact-phase split parity is also exact for three additional 1 ms steps after the source-validated 0.58 s pressure endpoint. `from_pressure_report` imports only the same-pressure-law exact-source report, verifies fresh initial lineage and the accepted final clock/state/ledger, and reconstructs potential and kinetic energy. It rejects legacy point-law reports. No capture claim.
