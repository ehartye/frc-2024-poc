# Two hypothetical transverse cassette mechanisms

This additive fixture combines two pressure/cam/spring mechanisms with one NOTE free along Y. Each side has independent inward travel, cam angle, velocities and mechanical torque input. Positive side uses the existing source-derived force geometry; the negative side is a mathematical reflection, **not adopted negative-side CAD**. Its actual pitch-chain routing, mounts, clearance and actuator hardware remain unresolved.

The local reflection maps NOTE y/velocity and material-grid phase signs. Inward travel is positive on both mechanisms. Forces are polar vectors; reported constraint moments are axial vectors. The shared NOTE kinetic energy is counted once. Separate spring, cam and sampled material-pressure potentials are recomputed every RK4 stage. Pressure reaction is computed from actual sampled geometry, never replaced by spring force. NOTE X/Z/orientation and cradle are fixed constraints, whose nonzero reactions do zero work only because their velocities are zero.

The eight bounded cases use a 1024-by-1024 material grid: symmetric external holding torque, its half-step version, torque removal, zero spring preload, zero-preload half-step, a 100 micrometre initial-offset domain control, and asymmetric preload/torque with its half-step version. Additional 512/1024 and quarter-cell phase queries expose discretization variation. Initial energy is declared, not imported from acquisition. Component masses, inertias, pressure density and cam compliance/damping are explicit inherited hypotheses. There is no friction, gravity, floor, rotor or electrical servo model. An external bounded mechanical torque is not a command that sets actual angle. Moving end-stop impacts remain unsupported and reject atomically; static zero-speed reactions do zero work.

Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_two_cassette*.py -v` and `python sim/pressure/run_two_cassette.py artifacts/fresh-two-cassette.json`. Fresh output paths required. Source inventories include the one-sided implementation and all its registered dependencies. No capture, lift, passive latch or hardware acceptance follows from this fixture.

## Recorded result
Seven supported cases reach the 2 ms horizon. Symmetric holding preserves NOTE y=0, but does not hold cassette displacement fixed. Torque removal preserves state and backdrives the cams. The asymmetric preload case develops negative NOTE velocity without prescribing it. Symmetric maximum energy residual decreases from 0.7714 nJ to 0.02450 nJ on halving the time step. Zero-preload residual decreases from 1.6497 to 0.2944 microjoules; asymmetric residual decreases from 0.8252 to 0.1472 microjoules. These short refinements are not a general convergence certificate.

The 100 micrometre initial-offset case is UNKNOWN_INITIAL_DOMAIN: the fine grid encounters unsupported deep/cavity contact. A coarse 128 grid does not expose this boundary, so coarse passing unit fixtures must not expand the law's domain. No state is accepted or advanced for that case. The archived receipt retains it alongside all supported outcomes. Finite pressure-grid/phase controls remain diagnostic, not calibrated contact area or spatial convergence.
