# Driving, aiming and feeding

During the trip to a NOTE, the controller computes a field-relative translation toward the staging point and a heading error toward the pickup orientation. It rotates the translation into robot coordinates. Forward speed, lateral speed and turn rate reach the four modules in the same control update. During loaded travel the robot also controls the lift and cradle pitch. At the shooting station it holds position and heading while feeding. [Approach controller](../sim/pressure/swerve_next_note.py) · [Module plant](../sim/pressure/maxswerve.py) · [Station controller](../sim/pressure/wing_note_release.py)

In the paired first-shot development runs the common command used 3,950 rpm, a 0.8 launcher duty limit, 0.23 pinch duty, −0.117° pitch trim and +0.84 mm height trim over 0.30 s. Those inputs belong to these trials. A change to wheel contact sampling produced misses. A successful simulated release requires the retained NOTE to separate with upward velocity, cross the opening, and then register at both modeled sensor pairs. Keep the chassis, module, launcher and free-NOTE states on the same clock across stages. [Shot command and outcomes](../sim/pressure/evidence/maxswerve-development-shot.json) · [Carry continuity checks](../sim/pressure/verify_swerve_carry.py)
