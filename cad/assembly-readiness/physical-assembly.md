The complete upper lift package ends at y = ±303.576 mm. Initially, 34 other bodies projected beyond the front, with a maximum of 56.278 mm. Every installed non-bumper body bounding box fits within the structural polygon, including the projecting carrier with 3.722 mm forward clearance. Whole-robot height and wire exposure should be checked on the assembled machine. [Native hull and body screen](frame-fit.json) · [2024 R101, R102 and R104](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2024/Manual/HTML/2024GameManual.htm#R101)

Fixture the outside faces to ±1.00 mm in each overall dimension. The high side of both tolerances adds 4.00 mm to the rectangular perimeter, leaving 5.351 mm to 3048 mm. After welding and cooling, remove the bumpers, put the mechanism in the recorded parked pose, measure both diagonals and overall spans, and pull a taut nonstretch line at bumper height. Reject a frame above 3048 mm or a parked article that touches a vertical straightedge ahead of the frame. Check cable ties, boots and leads in their installed condition.

### Removable bumper

The two front and two rear foam cores are 749.30 mm long. The four side cores and both side boards are 846.2245 mm long. Front and rear boards are 711.20 mm long. The exact 12 parent solids were restored from the registered private R7 before modifying the end geometry. The old solid cosmetic cover occupied 35.69 L. Each foam core now uses the supplier's nominal 2 lb/ft³ closed-cell polyethylene. Weigh the foam and finished boards from the selected lot. [Core transfer record](bumper-core-volumes.json) · [AndyMark solid foam](https://andymark.com/products/solid-pool-noodle)

For each face, pin the two barrel nuts in their square pockets, seat the two cores and wrap the cloth around the outside, top and bottom. Nine Arrow T50 506 staple locations per board edge give 72 anchors. Drive staples into the middle of the plywood edge and inspect crown seating and tip containment on an offcut first. The drawing assumes a straight-sided skin. One continuous blank with a 12.7 mm allowance on each side of one seam and two 12.7 mm hems is approximately 3.737 × 0.322 m, about 1.20 m² before trimming. Mock up the folded corners and measure the retained cloth. Installed textile mass equals retained area times measured kg/m² plus thread, marking and staple mass. AndyMark specifies 1000-denier nylon. The nominal shell material density alone cannot weigh woven cloth. [Bumper fabrics](https://andymark.com/products/bumper-fabrics) · [Staple catalog](https://arrowfastener.com/wp-content/uploads/Arrow-Fastener-Product-Catalog-2020.pdf)

Seat the covering before marking numbers. Check the harder backing at the corners and actual fit of the cloth over the adjacent foam ends. Nominal screw engagements are 8.425 mm front, 8.188 mm rear and 9.650 mm side. Respective tip-to-outer-face distances are 3.575, 3.812 and 2.350 mm. For the side joints, a 0.75 mm combined adverse board thickness/compression change, 0.25 mm screw-length increase and 0.15 mm washer reduction leave 1.20 mm tip clearance. Inspect actual grip and reject a projecting tip or an unseated flange. Grip changes in the opposite direction reduce engagement by the same stack. The commercial nominal 3/4 inch designation permits different actual thicknesses. Measure the stock before routing. [Eight seat measurements](fit.json) · [Screw geometry](https://us.misumi-ec.com/pdf/mold/15e_mo1185.pdf)

Support all four boards on a fixture. Remove all eight screws and four loose side washers, leave the captive barrels in the boards, then raise the chassis 200 mm. The reopened model checked every parked solid against the continuous swept panel, foam, cover and numeral bounds. All eight tool gauges and the removal stroke cleared. Time and ease of physical removal, fabric abrasion, dented tubes and cable movement need a shop check. [Service sweep](fit.json)
