# Launcher spacing and carrier mounting revision

The 49.2 mm finished-tread gap is the selected nominal candidate. It preserves 0.8 mm geometric indentation per side for a 50.8 mm thick rigid NOTE while separating the aligned bare-wheel flange by approximately 0.47 mm. Tolerances, wear, compression forces and traction remain unmeasured. Prior launch speeds and scoring results are not transferred.

The full cartridge interface audit exposed inherited interference that earlier NOTE and moving-mechanism screens did not cover. Both the 46.8 mm baseline and the gap-only alternative had the same 30 raw intersections per key pose. The larger gap introduced no new pair, but that did not establish completed mounts.

Original corrections:
- Move each support web behind the carrier's rear edge, ending at its X=160 mm face rather than overlapping its plate. Terminate at the side rail's inner face. Upper and lower webs meet at the center plane rather than overlapping one another. These are proposed edge-weld interfaces, not sized or qualified welds.
- Enlarge the motor-side bridge aperture from radius10.65 to14.65 mm. The nominal pulley envelope radius13.144 plus1 mm slider travel fits with0.506 mm nominal radial reserve. This is not manufacturing tolerance acceptance.
- End each tension-jack tip at the motor-slider outside face rather than penetrating1 mm into it. Major-diameter screw overlap inside the declared tap-drill bore remains a simplified thread representation.

The corrected three-pose audit retains every raw overlap. Sixteen remain: four conservative wheel-shell/tread proxy overlaps, eight pulley/pitch-band engagement proxies, and four jack/tapped-lug thread proxies. No carrier-web, bridge/driver-pulley or jack-tip/slider volume intersections remain. Native minimum-distance queries confirm all eight web-to-carrier/rail relationships still meet at zero gap. Zero gap alone does not establish contact area, weld strength or assembly access.

This is not a completed production mounting design. Remaining gates include weld preparation and sizing, fastener preload and locking, shaft/collar retention, chain attachment and tension, motor/gearbox mount details, wire/tool service access and measured compliance. Fresh full-piece transfer and six-drive simulation must use this geometry and independent force assumptions.
