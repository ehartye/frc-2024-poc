# Upper portal and carriage connections

This additive candidate operates on the lower-support-equipped part list without changing frozen canonical exports. Two original 3.175 mm drilled gusset plates connect the guide tops to the upper transverse portal. Two original proposed welded U-yokes span the actual lateral gaps from carriage plates to pivot drop brackets. Each interface has actual registered bores in both mating members and selected nominal screw/head/nut stacks.

There are 52 added solids: four original connecting parts and sixteen three-piece fastener stacks. Upper gusset material stacks are 6.35 mm, yoke stacks 9.525 mm. The existing sourced #10-32 x 19.05 mm screw/nut representations are used; threads/preload are not simulated. Internal tube nuts need installation before closing the portal. Yoke drop bolts point outward to keep protruding tips away from the shooter carriers.

The first yoke bolt orientation produced a real sampled lift collision with the positive upper shooter carrier (maximum 3.13 mm3). That rejected report is preserved. Revised stacks have no raw intersections against the assembly across 15 sampled poses. This does not establish continuous clearance or structural capacity. Weldment connected-solid and undrilled-guide negative controls are separate tests.

Attachment chain now represented by these two additive modules: source rail adapter -> authored bridge stub -> lower bracket -> guide -> upper portal gusset; carriage plate -> U-yoke -> drop bracket -> pivot bearing. The carriage pad attachment, pivot torque hub/retention, chain attachment/tensioning, motor mounting and remaining shooter rail connections are still incomplete. No claim that this is a complete load path or qualified robot.

Run `python cad/assembly/upper_carriage_connections.py`. It preserves exact base dependency pins and its lower-support dependency hash. These are original proposals and nominal hardware representations, not redistributed supplier geometry.
