# Explore the 2024 robot

Choose **R7 · nominal mass D1 replay** to inspect the straight approach. Use **Pickup**, **Release**, **Opening** and **Counter** to pause the model at the measured events. The counter view shows the first NOTE at 13.639 s. The historical first-shot film below has its own chapter times. [Open the nominal R7 animation](https://ehartye.github.io/frc-2024-poc/?view=r7-budget#model) · [Same-archive observation](../sim/pressure/evidence/r7-first-note-D1.json)

1. Open the [robot showcase](https://ehartye.github.io/frc-2024-poc/#model). In the initial three-quarter view, identify the red 1780 bumper, the floor intake and the upright lift. Drag to orbit, or focus the model and use the arrow keys.
2. Choose **View four swerve modules**. Find each fork and the wheel it carries. Start **Play pickup and shot**, pause during the return, and compare the four headings. **Reset view** returns to the whole robot. [Underside view](https://ehartye.github.io/frc-2024-poc/?view=swerve#model)
3. Scrub the replay through lift, pitch, feed and release. The orange NOTE starts on the floor. Follow it through the opening at 10.705 s, then choose **Inspect sensor event** to see the ring at both sensor pairs at 12.915 s. Compare the times with the [shot record](../sim/pressure/evidence/maxswerve-development-shot.json).
4. Select the six-wheel baseline in the revision picker. Watch the [full-route film](https://ehartye.github.io/frc-2024-poc/#route) and locate the sixth sensor event. The baseline and swerve recordings describe different runs.
5. For an editable session in Fusion, use the authorized [native design handoff](../cad/native-handoff.md). For the loaded drive-and-turn question, inspect the [controls record](../controls/swerve-and-feed.md).
