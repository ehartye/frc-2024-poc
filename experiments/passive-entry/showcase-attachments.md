## Trace the passive intake and frame

* On R9 the outside tread 534 touches backing belt 504. The latter reaches pulley 506, stub shaft 505, bearing envelope 507 and side weldment 536. The other side follows 535 → 519 → 521 → 520 → 522 → 538. The weldments reach pivots 537/539 and the carriage's bearing representations 251/264. The two tread paths also appear on R7. Surface contact locates these articles. Axial retention of the bearings, stub shafts and pulleys still needs a section and a retained hardware stack.
* Motor 513 and plate 514 form a two-shell group. Motor 528 and plate 529 form another. Neither has a displayed attachment path to a weldment. The rotation motor and gearbox envelopes 274/275 also form their own two-shell group. The R17 passive support mounts directly to 536/538. Crediting a functioning legacy powered intake from these images would exceed the evidence.
* Every drive tire touches only its hub. All four hub/tire pairs are separate from their axle bosses in both views. Each fork/azimuth carrier reaches its lower housing and ears. At each corner that 16-shell group is separate from the tab-mounted rectangular rails. The native study registers steering and rolling joints. The frame build sheet calls for twelve 10-32 through-bolts, sleeves and locknuts at actual module tabs. The public simplified corners omit the complete supplier axle, keeper and tab geometry. Inspect the exact purchased revision and interfaces before accepting wheel retention. [Frame build sheet](../../cad/drivetrain/MAXSWERVE_FRAME.md) · [Native versus display parts](../../cad/native-handoff.md)
* R9 frame rails 540, 550 and 555 belong to the 330-shell frame and electrical group. The cradle and portal are in a different 227-shell group. R9 documents four welded nose spacers and fitted weld faces. A visible route through a rail or an upper portal gusset establishes position only. This audit does not qualify weld strength, bearing fits or bolt preload. [R9 frame fit and drawings](../../cad/assembly-readiness/physical-assembly.md)

## Account for the other separate groups

The [machine-readable inventory](showcase-audit.json) includes every shell's visual node, bounds in robot millimeters, any matching R9 bill names, every measured edge and every connected group. In R9 the group sizes are 330, 227, two of 75, four of 16, one of 3, thirteen of 2 and two singletons. They include two separate 75-shell side assemblies, the sliding member, four historical wheel-envelope pairs, the front drive motors with caps, and individual controller and locking-pin shapes. R7 also has a separate radio and deck, individual deck/electrical shapes, and a distinct 11-shell frame group. These counts include intended moving clearance, simplified hardware, incidental touching and missing mounting detail. A missing surface path is an open display finding until the intended joint or fastener is identified in the complete revision.

The [audit](showcase-audit.mjs) follows the active scene hierarchy, composes the first animation keys with the node transforms, and converts to robot coordinates. Within each primitive it welds coincident vertices to a millionth of a millimeter, splits shells and tests candidate triangle pairs. An edge requires an intersection or a vertex/face or edge/edge separation of at most 0.20 mm. Bounds only prune candidates and suggest names from the [registered R9 bill](registered-boxes.json). Shells may merge at coincident vertices. Enclosed separate surfaces with a running clearance may stay disconnected. The test considers the displayed pose and tessellation only. It cannot identify a thread, bond, weld, joint constraint or retention capacity from contact alone.
