# Removable slide motor

Install the purchased motor on the cap at the bench, bolt the receiver to the prepared slide bearing block, then insert the motor and cap together along the spindle axis. Two accessible vertical fasteners retain the cap on a pair of shoulders. The bearing block remains on the frame.

The original straight driver path to each motor screw intersects the installed bearing mount. The first receiver pattern put one pilot on the edge. The revised pattern puts both full 8 mm pilots in solid material. The archived fit, the two measured hole volumes, the 75 mm driver paths, the first 45 mm of axial removal and the retained-screw negative are in fit.json and initial-edge-pilot.json. The source-bound fixture is slide-cartridge.step.

The proposed aluminum receiver and cap weigh 48.8 g by volume. Six modeled steel fastening envelopes add 10.2 g. Treat these as additions until the complete slide purchase list is reconciled. The clamp screws, actual cable disconnect, bearing thrust rating, machining margins and final route out of the robot remain open. motion.json covers moving stock at the sampled pickup, return, service and endgame poses.
