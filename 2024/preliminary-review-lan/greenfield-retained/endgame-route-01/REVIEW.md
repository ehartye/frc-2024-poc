# 2024 preliminary design decision

The active robot uses the Profile A removable lip and separate feed and launch belt zones. Keep detailed design on hold. The two remaining mission decisions are six-NOTE repeatability and the solo ONSTAGE load path.

A 100 mm guided stroke at the capture angle gives 96.765 mm of vertical separation in the ideal layout. All 180 installed bodies were checked against 3,894 field and peer bounds over this straight path. The working chain produces candidates at the two hooks and keeper mast. At five evenly spaced strokes there was zero positive overlap of the seven moving hook and keeper articles with the retained robot. The separate loaded trial tests how a freely suspended chassis departs from the assumed straight path. [Field sweep](guided-field.json) · [Package screen](guided-hooks.json)

The published baseline has only 0.199 kg headroom at the upper mass estimate. The guided-pull study charges an extra 0.750 kg for a provisional moving group, so carrying all existing hardware plus that allocation exceeds the baseline allowance by 0.551 kg. Reconcile replaced members, actual slide stock, drive, mechanical stops and wiring before selecting production hardware. The 8.272 V loaded-drive result belongs to the baseline drive case. The guide needs its own current and mechanical power check.

## Loaded guided pull

At 100 mm and 170 mm stroke the frontward CG case reloaded a tire during the disabled hold. The 170 mm rearward CG held, while disabling the positive stop returned the chassis to the carpet. At 220 mm, with the keeper drive, return pinion, mast, crossbar, rack, motor and battery geometry included, the solved chassis stayed off the floor at both longitudinal CG extremes (±40 mm). Minimum sampled clearance from motor cutoff through five seconds was 4.80 and 73.05 mm. Both hooks carried positive load in both runs. The adverse case misses the inherited 10 mm preliminary allowance. The frame pitch lock, carriage guide and ratchet remain mechanical design assumptions. Zero drive power during this simulated hold does not rate them for fabrication.
