# Profile A preliminary risk experiment 01

Freeze the default blade and parent assembly 83517673. Three independent screens. Retain all failed trials. Stop after the declared cases and a single numerical refinement of governing outcomes. No purchases, fabrication, robot deployment or Fusion work.

* **Pickup.** Add 0.75° nose-up at the deployed stop. Screen ±0.5° pitch and −2/+3 mm chassis height, grip 0.45/0.75 and lateral offsets up to 50 mm. Require at least 1 mm blade clearance on the rigid floor, free NOTE seated for 40 ms, and positive modeled flap clearance. Use a finite-traction motor for the center belt/rollers and trial 8 A and 2 A limits. Keep a drive-off and original pitch control. Side belt traction retains the previous capped contact law. The bench decision requires actual loaded speed, carpet seams and worn NOTEs.
* **Climb.** Compare the existing angle-window latch with a finite-torque spring-applied friction brake at the pivot that responds to power loss at any angle. Test −40, 0, +40 mm longitudinal CG, 180 N·m capacity, 30 ms delay, 30 ms linear engagement; challenge reduced capacity, delayed engagement and no brake. Require two captured hooks, zero motor torque after cutoff, less than 3° pivot drift, at least 10 mm modeled ground clearance and no ground support through the five-second disabled interval. Measure brake torque. Initial hook capture remains idealized. No hardware rating is inferred.
* **Shot.** Enforce a 100 ms uninterrupted permission window before belt enable. Forecast 100 A additional pack current, require predicted bus ≥8.2 V and drivetrain acceleration released. Check through transient and permanent drive demand and an ungated control. After enable inhibit renewed acceleration through the shot. Check ≥8 V throughout, resolved finite-ring opening and both detector shadows on the same shot. Weak pack 11.8 V, 27 mΩ. Compare coarse/fine time steps. A denied shot while acceleration persists is an intentional inhibit and costs a scoring opportunity.

Any layout or control improvement remains provisional until its model and physical limitations are reviewed. Keep the review's current authority and existing animations identifiable.
