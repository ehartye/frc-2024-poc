# 2024 strategic narrative

## Mission before mechanism

Explore a close-range Speaker cycle specialist: start with one retained NOTE, drive to a repeatable shot pose, release once, acquire one known floor NOTE, retract/index, return and release again. The current desktop candidate completes this serialized investigation in about 27.3 seconds. That is not a 15-second autonomous routine, official scoring or match-winning evidence.

## Alternatives retained

| Narrative | Why consider it | Why not first |
|---|---|---|
| Close Speaker cycles | Existing target geometry and powered-release evidence allow a complete acquisition-to-release experiment | Floor capture, traffic, real launch attitude and housing clearance remain uncertain |
| Amp-focused placement/support | Different role and lower-speed placement tradeoff | Requires a separate verified placement/lift path; Speaker evidence does not transfer |
| Feeder/defense support | Could prioritize acquisition, delivery and traffic reliability | Value depends on alliance coordination and contested-field simulation not yet implemented |

Choose the Speaker investigation provisionally, not as an irreversible build decision. Reconsider if measured capture reliability, cycle time, opponent exposure or release repeatability defeats the role.

## Derived design

A differential chassis with continuous bumper envelopes carries a separate reversible pickup/indexer and opposed-wheel shooter. Separating capture from release enables different contact operating points and clearer recovery observations, at the cost of parts and mass. A shared reversible intake/shooter remains a meaningful alternative; no team CAD is copied. Prior designs inform attributed engineering lessons, not geometry replication.

Spatial screening rejected a short intake that intersected the bumper, a longer fixed arm that exceeded extension during motion, and an extension-only telescope that exceeded height. The current height-capped telescope and level cradle are original prescribed-kinematic hypotheses. Their 4.8 mm forward and 5 mm height allowances are nominal, not tolerances or structural approval.

## Software and team collaboration

The baseline serializes drive, acquire/index/stow and stopped release. It surfaces stale/unknown observations and low shooter RPM, allows one bounded acquisition retry and jam reversal, and preserves explicit operator-requested degraded planning without inventing a shorter accurate shot. Concurrent driving/spinup, moving release and aiming under defense remain experiments, not automatic time savings. Designers own path/support/service geometry; programmers own observable states and fault handling; fabricators contribute available processes, materials and adjustment/repeatability constraints.

## Falsifiable next tests

- Replace prescribed floor seating/retention with tested contact and actuation models, including new/worn piece dimensions, stiffness and friction ranges.
- Resolve complete target roof/walls and derive body spin/attitude from contact rather than assuming fixed guide alignment.
- Add collision-aware field structures and scripted opposing robots; footprint exposure is not impact strength.
- Measure cycle distributions, failures and recovery cost, not only one nominal completion.
- Test service access, repeatable adjustment, power-loss behavior and load paths; keep records in reliability/pit-plan.json.
