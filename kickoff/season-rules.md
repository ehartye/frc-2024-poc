# 2024 CRESCENDO rules register

This study uses the archived final 2024 rules. Team Update 21 was the final published update. An early-season robot inspection would require its event's edition. The historical kickoff register retains the section identifiers, source fingerprints and open hardware questions. [Final manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2024/Manual/HTML/2024GameManual.htm) · [Team Update 21](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2024/Manual/TeamUpdates/TeamUpdate21.pdf) · [Rule reading map](historical/rule-reading-map.json) · [Hardware register](historical/hardware-rule-catalog.json)

| Game decision | Authority | Consequence for this robot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specialize in close Speaker cycles | §§6.5.1, 6.5.3, 6.5.6 | Ordinary TELEOP Speaker scores earn 2 points and amplified scores earn 5. Budget the floor route without relying on amplification. |
| Acquire one floor NOTE at a time | §6.3.4, G403, G409, G410 | Start the six-piece exercise empty. Give each NOTE a distinct identity and trace it through intake, retention, release and field processing. |
| Keep a continuous front bumper | R401–R403, R408, R410 | Route the NOTE under the front bumper. Check its swept volume, the backing and removable mounts separately. |
| Fit the starting configuration and moving envelope | G413 and the robot construction rules | Check the lift and launcher in native robot coordinates. The starting-size audit still shows a lift-motor and launcher-rail overhang. |
| Credit the score at the counter | §6.5.1 and official counter assessment | Record opening passage and the later counter event for the same NOTE. Verify the counter can distinguish the next NOTE. |

The practice interval is 120 simulated seconds. Official TELEOP lasts 135 seconds. Floor acquisition, preload and SOURCE introduction have different entry conditions. The proposed overhead receiving route remains a separate design question.
