## Taking the STAGE

The release-day manual and final register agree on these values. A parked robot earns 1 point. An ONSTAGE robot earns 3, or 4 if the microphone for its supporting chain is SPOTLIT. Two ONSTAGE robots sharing one chain earn 2 additional HARMONY points in total. Three earn 4 additional points. A TRAP can contribute 5 for one NOTE. Assess the alliance at the earlier of all robots coming to rest after TELEOP or five seconds after the buzzer. Drivers have 135 powered TELEOP seconds. G424 protection and G430 HIGH NOTE entry apply in the final 20 seconds. A lawful approach can begin earlier. [Final manual, §§5.5, 6.4–6.5.2, Table 6-2](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2024/Manual/HTML/2024GameManual.htm) · [Kickoff V0 comparison and final scoring register](stage-rules.json)

For PARK, check bumper overlap with the team's STAGE ZONE and rule out ONSTAGE. For ONSTAGE, check every contact against §6.5.2, including contact with the floor. HARMONY groups robots by their supporting chain, including transitive support. Check its microphone separately. ENSEMBLE is 1 qualification ranking point once the alliance has at least 10 STAGE match points and two ONSTAGE robots. A G424 referee award has a separate basis. The lab prescribes terminal observations, and the engineering study records which ones the robot can currently demonstrate. [Three match results](../experiments/speaker-cycles/match-rehearsal.md)

The practice interval is 120 simulated seconds. Official TELEOP lasts 135 seconds. Floor acquisition, preload and SOURCE introduction have different entry conditions. The proposed overhead receiving route remains a separate design question.
