# Score from the floor at close range

The 2024 robot specializes in collecting a loose NOTE and delivering it to the blue Speaker. Its alliance contribution depends on how often it can repeat the entire trip. We begin with drive-over pickup under the continuous front bumper. The angled output may also receive an overhead NOTE. That route still needs a defined entry pose, capture geometry and retention evidence.

### Match sequence

1. Start empty. Approach a known floor mark with the cradle lowered and the intake facing the NOTE.
2. Seat the NOTE between the powered tracks. When the simulator observes sustained bilateral contact and suitable relative velocity, carry it away.
3. Return toward the close shooting station while turning toward the Speaker and raising the mechanism. In paired swerve development runs, strafe, turn and lift overlapped for 2.65 s and 2.64 s.
4. Reach the station, stabilize, spin the launcher to speed and feed. Follow the same NOTE through the opening until both modeled sensor pairs register it. Begin the next pickup while the field processes the previous NOTE.

The six-wheel baseline reached its sixth sensor event at 118.7945 s. Swerve is the selected drivetrain. Compare it with that baseline using the same piece supply and clock. The target is six scores in 120 s on at least 19 of 20 frozen starts. Neither of the two swerve development starts enters that denominator. ### Competitive context

In the two 2024 championship finals the winning blue alliance scored 11 and 9 TELEOP Speaker NOTES. Across 135 seconds those are one Speaker NOTE every 12.27 and 15.00 seconds. They are three-robot alliance totals. Match breakdowns do not identify each robot's scoring cadence. Six ordinary scores in the project's 120-second interval would yield 0.10 Speaker points/s from this robot. If all six were amplified they would yield 0.25 points/s and require alliance coordination. Both figures are conditional on completing the route. [Final 1](https://frc-events.firstinspires.org/2024/CMPTX/playoffs/14) · [Final 2](https://frc-events.firstinspires.org/2024/CMPTX/playoffs/15)
