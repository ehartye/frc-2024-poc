# Six-robot CRESCENDO match rehearsal

Use a blue and a red alliance of three robots. These are plausible role assignments for a strategy discussion. A robot can attempt its assigned task and miss. The physics evidence for the 1780 design is linked separately. [Scoring mission](../experiments/speaker-cycles/mission.md)

| Station | AUTO, 0–15 s | TELEOP, 15–150 s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue 1 · 1780 close Speaker | Attempt the preload from its starting shot, then approach the middle wing NOTE. | Pick up from the floor, turn and raise during the return, stop at the near shooting station, and feed. Continue toward the next available piece. |
| Blue 2 · AMP partner | Attempt one preload placement at the AMP and leave the starting area. | Run short AMP deliveries. Coordinate an amplification window when eligible and the Speaker partner is ready. |
| Blue 3 · midfield feeder | Leave toward a separate center-line lane and attempt to retrieve the nearest available NOTE. | Deliver available NOTES toward the home scoring side and leave a clear return lane. |
| Red 1 · Speaker cycler | Attempt a preload and a short wing pickup from its own starting side. | Return floor NOTES to a repeatable close shot. Take a free wing NOTE before making a longer center-line trip. |
| Red 2 · AMP / Speaker support | Attempt a preload from a stationary pose, then clear the path for Red 1. | Supply the AMP when amplification is useful and switch to short Speaker cycles when a lane is open. |
| Red 3 · midfield disruptor | Leave by a different center approach and attempt a free pickup. | Contest loose midfield NOTES through positioning and possession. Yield protected areas and retain an exit path. |

For a series, use the same six role definitions across nominal, shared-midfield and missed-pickup scenarios. Compare each alliance's score events, missed attempts, contested pieces and final possession. Run AUTO through the transition and TELEOP to the final whistle. At 10×, 150 game seconds occupy 15 seconds of playback. Group robots by alliance even if the operator adds all three red robots before adding blue.

The field rehearsal models tactical choices and timing. The independent [paired swerve physics records](../sim/pressure/evidence/maxswerve-development-shot.json) track contact-driven release and individual score sensors. Their scope is narrower than a six-robot match.

## End-of-match decision

At TELEOP 111 s in the shared-chain rehearsal, Blue 1 leaves the Speaker route and Blue 2 leaves its assigned lane. Stage them 1,000 mm apart along one chain, facing away from the core. Leave the second robot a clear approach and confirm the correct microphone. Two unlit hangs plus HARMONY yield 8 STAGE points. Spotlighting that chain makes 10 with both robots ONSTAGE.
